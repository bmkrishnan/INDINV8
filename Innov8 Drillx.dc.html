The problem

Most organisations are underprepared — and they know it.

Cyber tabletop exercises are how security teams test whether they can respond to an attack before a real one hits. Today they're run manually, led by external consultants, and cost tens of thousands per session — so most organisations run one a year, or none, and find their gaps during a live incident instead of a drill.

The solution

Cyber readiness, on demand.

Drillx turns the tabletop exercise from a costly annual event into a continuous capability. Set up a realistic scenario in minutes, run it with your team from any browser, and walk away with a board-ready report — no external facilitator required. Practise as often as you need, against the threats that actually matter to your organisation.
