About Ind Innov8

India's hub for research, development, and expertise.

Ind Innov8 Private Limited is an India-based, DPIIT-recognised technology company. We exist to turn applied research into shipped software — engineering AI-native products from day one, not bolting AI on afterward.

Cyber security is our proving ground: Drillx, our cyber drill platform, is where that research and engineering discipline meets a real, unsolved problem. Alongside our own products, our subject-matter experts work directly with enterprises across the ASEAN region to design AI roadmaps and adopt AI responsibly, in collaboration with affiliated Innov8 entities in Singapore and Brunei.
